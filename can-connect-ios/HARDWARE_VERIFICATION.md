# Hardware acceptance

Build 7 (1.0.0) includes the Android Pocket Recorder's standard Jetta readings and cancellation cleanup while preserving the locked design. On October 23, 2026, the real 2014 Jetta TDI DSG/MX+ supplied 13 supported Mode 01 PIDs and 20 available fields. Native UI checks displayed RPM, boost, DPF pressure, regeneration trigger and regeneration status. A 76-second capture (821 responses) and a later 21.8-second capture (224 responses) transferred successfully. Phone and Mac originals, SHA-256, ECU metadata, timestamps, vehicle binding and all processed fields matched; the phone shows Complete.

An interrupted connection exposed a reconnect failure. Build 7 drains an in-flight response, awaits canceled work even after explicit Disconnect, and reuses the foreground MFi session. The underlying session closes on backgrounding or transport failure. Regression tests cover cancellation during opening and pending reads. Three physical disconnect/reconnect cycles supplied fresh RPM without another Bluetooth reset, followed by a new recording/transfer check. The latest counts and evidence are recorded in PR #7. Longer driving, calibrated accuracy and interruption/recovery acceptance remain outstanding; see JETTA_2014.md. The native Mac importer was updated separately to recognize all measured fields, and retained originals were reprocessed without changing trip IDs or original bytes.

Earlier build 4 checks passed a stationary physical GPS recording and a short MX+ recording, both transferred to the native Mac app with exact original bytes, hashes, timestamps, receipt and vehicle binding verified. Original phone/Mac histories were retained privately. These checks do not establish moving-route accuracy, calibrated sensor accuracy or extended-drive reliability.

Use a parked vehicle for initial checks. Keep personal VINs, routes and pairing tokens outside Git and public issue logs.

1. **Install/sign:** Build 7 is installed as CAN Connect. For subsequent builds, choose your development team in Xcode and run CANConnect.xcodeproj on the iPhone. Confirm onboarding/profile persistence after relaunch. The generic unsigned Release build is a build check; only a development-signed build can be installed on the provisioned device. See IPHONE_TESTING.md.
2. **GPS:** grant location access, obtain a real fix, record movement, lock/unlock the phone, and finish. Confirm coordinates, timestamps, accuracy, gaps and recovery behavior in the exported recording. Denial/revocation should show an actionable state; a sample route is not GPS acceptance.
3. **Scanner compatibility:** join the actual Wi-Fi ELM327 network; enter its documented host/port. Verify required AT settings, prompt framing, VIN/protocol, supported PIDs and sensible standard readings with ignition/engine state. Compare units and values with a trusted scan tool. Vehicles without VIN support require manual confirmation. A mismatched VIN must block acquisition. For build 7, also test OBDLink MX+ via Bluetooth/MFi using MXPLUS.md; basic pairing, session, live readings and short recording were verified; repeat connection and extended-drive acceptance remain pending.
4. **Recording/freshness:** record several minutes, stop, relaunch, and compare raw commands/replies and timestamps with displayed readings. Turn ignition off or disconnect the scanner transport; stale readings should clear and the draft should save. Backgrounding saves/stops scanner acquisition; screen lock/background scanning is not supported. Reopen and reconnect explicitly. Verify unavailable PIDs stay blank. Validate rate/coverage before relying on calculated fuel totals.
5. **Two vehicles:** confirm separate histories/preferences, correct scanner identity and trip vehicle ID, and prevention of switching during recording. Reconnecting must identify the newly chosen vehicle.
6. **Mac LAN:** explicitly enable LAN access in the native Mac app and satisfy macOS firewall/local-network permissions. Join the same reachable private network from the phone, grant iOS local-network access, then pair using the real token. Test `.local` and private IPv4 URLs. Do not configure public exposure for this check.
7. **Transfer:** send a GPS and scanner recording. Check the Mac's original files, vehicle binding, actual values, receipt hash/bytes and processed trip. Check the phone retains its originals. Test a wrong token, unreachable Mac, interrupted upload, queued/paused processing and retry after relaunch. There should be one Mac original/receipt for identical retries; a queued receipt must not display completion. Scanner Wi-Fi commonly lacks access to the home Mac; switch networks after recording.
8. **Resource behavior:** measure scanner latency, battery/heat, larger recording checkpoints and upload duration. Test the 100,000-response session limit, failed storage and large libraries on the physical phone before extended use.

Still outside this build: BLE, other MFi adapters, USB, raw CAN, manufacturer UDS/soot/DSG signals (standard Mode 01 DPF pressure and regeneration are supported), simultaneous GPS+OBD acquisition, automatic background uploads, kWh/charging logging, TestFlight/App Store distribution, and any public server deployment.
