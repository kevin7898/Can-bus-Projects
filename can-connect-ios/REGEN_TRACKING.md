# Automatic regeneration observations

Vehicle → DPF → Compare regeneration cycles shows the latest ten complete observations and a separate list of interrupted/incomplete observations. The first five are the initial comparison target. Export includes the displayed cycles, raw-reading timestamps and recording IDs. The start baseline is saved once on upgrade. Existing soot/percent-full calculations and scanner polling intervals are unchanged.

The analyzer derives observations from retained valid scanner responses for the selected vehicle. It requires an inactive status before start, two distinct active-status timestamps, and two successive inactive-status timestamps at the end. “Complete” describes an observed active-flag transition; it does not prove regeneration success or an empty filter. A gap over 20 seconds, recording end, recovered unfinished draft, or a nearby zero-RPM reading at the inactive transition remains incomplete. The latter protects against an ECU that continues answering briefly after engine stop. An observation beginning while already active is partial. Separate recordings are never joined across unobserved time; duplicate imports of the same trip ID do not multiply cycles. Demo/GPS records and unsupported status signals cannot create cycles.

Soot is the closest actual sample within 75 seconds; faster supporting readings must be within 10 seconds. Samples cannot cross a status-data gap. Details show whether each reading preceded or followed the boundary, so a delayed soot poll is never presented as an exact trigger mass. Peak exhaust is the maximum actual reading during the observed interval. Missing readings remain unavailable. No interpolation, capacity recalibration, forced regeneration, or ECU writes are performed.

History is rebuilt from the original local recordings after relaunch, so the originals are the source of truth and remain transferable to the Mac. Deleting a source recording also removes its derived observations. Export a comparison before deleting source recordings if you need a separate history. Cycle comparison UI currently lives on iPhone; Mac retains the raw recordings.
